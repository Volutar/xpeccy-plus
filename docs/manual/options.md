# Options

```{index} Options
```

```{index} see: preferences; Options
```

**Tools > Options...** opens the settings. The pages are listed on the left, and each one
has a page of its own here, in the same order.

{icon}`ok-apply` **OK** applies the changes and closes the window, **Apply** applies them
and keeps it open, {icon}`cancel` **Cancel** discards them. Hold the mouse over a setting to
see what it does. A click on a setting's name, or on the hint beside it, works
as a click on its check box; beside a list or a field, it puts the cursor there.
